2. SHIPPING & DELIVERY POLICIES
- Coverage: We deliver across all major cities in Pakistan including Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, and Peshawar.
- Delivery Timelines:
  * Standard Delivery in Lahore: 1 to 2 business days.
  * Major Cities (Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi): 2 to 4 business days.
  * Rest of Pakistan: 3 to 5 business days.
- Delivery Charges:
  * Free shipping on all orders over Rs. 3,000.
  * Flat fee of Rs. 200 for orders under Rs. 3,000.
- Cash on Delivery (COD): COD is available nationwide for orders up to Rs. 50,000. Orders above Rs. 50,000 require advance bank transfer or credit card payment.

3. RETURNS & EXCHANGE POLICY
- Return Window: Customers can request a return or exchange within 7 days of receiving the parcel.
- Return Eligibility:
  * Items must be unused, in original packaging, with all seals, tags, and accessories intact.
  * Change-of-mind returns are accepted within 7 days, provided the customer covers return shipping charges (Rs. 250).
- Damaged, Defective, or Wrong Items:
  * If a product arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, UrbanCart provides a 100% free replacement or full refund.
  * Customers must report damaged items within 24 hours of delivery by providing a photo/video proof.
- Non-Returnable Items: Personal care hygiene products, software keys, and customized accessories cannot be returned once opened.
- Refund Processing Time: Approved refunds are processed within 3 to 5 working days via Bank Transfer, EasyPaisa, or JazzCash.

4. WARRANTY & REPAIR POLICY
- Official Warranty: All electronics and appliances carry a standard 1-Year UrbanCart Official Warranty unless specified otherwise on the product page.
- Accessories Warranty: Mobile accessories (charging cables, adapters, power banks) carry a 6-Month Replacement Warranty.
- What is Covered: Manufacturing defects, internal hardware failures, and charging port issues.
- What is NOT Covered: Physical damage, liquid exposure, unauthorized repairs, or electrical surges.
- Warranty Claim Procedure: Customers can drop off the item at our Lahore Service Center or ship it back to us. Repairs/replacements take 7 to 10 business days.
